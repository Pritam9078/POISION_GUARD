# How do I create a Guard account and workspace?
Register with your name, email and a password of at least 10 characters, then verify your email from the link we send. Create a workspace; you become its owner and it starts on the Free plan. Invite teammates from Team & roles.

# How do I sign in with my wallet?
First sign in with email and link a wallet from settings. Linking asks your wallet to sign a Sign-In with Ethereum message that states it authorizes no transaction or approval; each message expires in 10 minutes and can be used only once. After linking you can sign in with that wallet. Smart-contract wallets (EIP-1271) are not supported for sign-in yet. A connected wallet never grants spending rights.

# I forgot my password or my account is locked
After 5 wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes. Use Forgot password to get a reset link valid for 1 hour; resetting signs you out of every session. Login errors are deliberately generic.

# How do invitations work?
Owners and admins invite by email with a role, optional message and expiry (1–30 days, default 7). The invite can only be accepted while signed in with that exact email. Invites count toward your plan's seats.

# Can I manage my sessions?
Yes: settings lists your active sessions; you can revoke one or all others. Changing your password revokes your other sessions.
