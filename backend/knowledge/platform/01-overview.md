# What is PoisonGuard?
PoisonGuard is a blockchain wallet security platform. It helps individuals and teams detect address poisoning, check a recipient before sending, review and revoke risky token approvals, and control spending by people and AI agents with policies, human approvals and a tamper-evident audit trail.
Core principle: analyze, explain, authorize and verify — never blindly execute. PoisonGuard is non-custodial: it never holds your private keys or seed phrase, never signs transactions for you and never broadcasts transactions. You always sign in your own wallet.

# What are the parts of the platform — the public site, Guard and the admin console?
The public website is free and needs no account: wallet and transaction scanner, recipient checker, approval revoke tool, methodology, accuracy, recovery guide, anti-phishing, report a finding and shareable risk cards.
Guard is the team workspace (sign-in required): watched wallets, trusted recipients, scan history, AI agents, spend intents, approval queue, policies and the policy simulator, team and roles, knowledge base, the assistant, audit timeline, proof explorer, security events, notifications and webhooks, billing, API keys and settings.
The platform admin console is used only by PoisonGuard staff. Staff cannot approve your payments, sign transactions, edit your policies or impersonate you.

# Who is PoisonGuard for?
Individual crypto users who want to check a wallet or recipient before sending. Treasury, finance and payroll teams, DAOs and startups that move funds and need approvals and audit evidence. Developers who run AI agents or bots that request on-chain spending and need hard limits.

# Does PoisonGuard guarantee that an address is safe?
No. PoisonGuard never labels anything "safe". A scan with no findings means no matching findings were detected in the data that was available. It does not prove the address is safe. Every result shows its data coverage so you can see what was and was not checked.

# Does PoisonGuard ever ask for my seed phrase or private key?
Never. PoisonGuard never holds, stores or asks for your private keys or seed phrase. No page, form, support agent or API ever asks for a seed phrase, recovery phrase or private key. Anyone who asks is impersonating PoisonGuard — report it on the anti-phishing page.
